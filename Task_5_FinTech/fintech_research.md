# FinTech Research Report: Zerodha

## 1. Introduction

Zerodha is an Indian financial technology company that provides online platforms for stock, mutual fund and other investment-related services. Its major products include Kite, Console, Coin and Kite Connect APIs.

## 2. How Zerodha Uses Data Analytics

Zerodha handles large volumes of financial and market data to provide users with useful information for monitoring their investments.

Its Console platform provides analytics and visualisations of trades and portfolios. It also processes historical trade information to calculate portfolio profit and loss while accounting for events such as corporate actions, splits and transfers.

## 3. Customer Experience

Data analytics improves the customer experience by converting complex financial data into understandable reports and visualisations.

Examples include:

* Portfolio performance tracking.
* Realised and unrealised P&L.
* Portfolio performance curves.
* Holdings and transaction reports.
* Tax-ready reports.
* Stock and portfolio insights.

Console's performance curve provides a visual representation of actual portfolio performance over time, while users can also analyse holdings and download reports.

Kite also provides market data, advanced charts, market depth and alerts, helping users analyse market information through a single platform.

## 4. Business Decisions and Operations

Analytics can support Zerodha's business operations by helping process large amounts of transaction and market data efficiently.

For example, its Console system processes historical trade information to generate accurate P&L calculations and reports. Such data processing is important for financial reporting, portfolio monitoring and regulatory requirements.

## 5. Data Analytics Perspective

From a Data Analyst's perspective, a FinTech platform such as Zerodha can involve:

* Data collection from transactions and market feeds.
* Data cleaning and transformation.
* Database storage.
* Financial calculations and aggregations.
* Performance and risk analysis.
* Data visualisation.
* Report generation.
* Monitoring and operational analytics.

These processes demonstrate how data analytics connects raw financial data with useful business and customer-facing insights.

## 6. Privacy and Responsible Data Use

An important aspect of Zerodha's approach is that it states that its mobile applications do not use direct or indirect customer-behaviour tracking or profiling, and that it does not use third-party marketing trackers for behavioural profiling.

Therefore, its use of analytics can be distinguished from behavioural advertising: much of the visible analytics functionality is focused on financial data, portfolio reporting, market information and operational functionality.

## 7. Conclusion

Zerodha demonstrates how Data Analytics can be applied in FinTech to transform large amounts of financial and market data into useful information. Features such as portfolio analytics, P&L reporting, performance visualisation, market data and downloadable reports improve transparency and help users understand their financial activity.

For a Data Analyst, this illustrates the complete process of turning raw financial data into reliable metrics, reports and visual insights that support both customers and business operations.
